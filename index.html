Frequently asked questions

Clear answers to the most common questions.

What exactly does Keyra do? +

Keyra is a coordination platform. We make it simple for everyday people to request clinician-reviewed peptide support for energy, recovery, and balance. We partner with independent licensed clinicians and authorized compounding pharmacies. Keyra itself does not prescribe, diagnose, or dispense medication.

How does the process work? +

1) You complete a short interest quiz. 2) When limited access opens, a licensed clinician through our partner network can review your information. 3) If appropriate, your request is sent to an authorized compounding pharmacy. 4) A discreet package is delivered to your door.

Is there any commitment or subscription? +

No. Joining the early-access list creates no obligation. When access is available, you only pay for what you choose to order. There are no memberships or automatic renewals.

Do you prescribe or ship the peptides yourselves? +

No. Keyra coordinates access only. We do not prescribe, dispense, or compound any medication. Prescribing decisions belong to independent licensed clinicians. Fulfillment is handled by authorized compounding pharmacies.

How private is my information? +

We take privacy seriously. The interest form collects only high-level information (no detailed medical history). We do not sell your personal information. See our full Privacy Policy.

What happens after I submit the quiz? +

You will be added to our early-access interest list. We will only contact you when limited spots become available or if we need a quick follow-up. No automatic enrollment and no commitment.
