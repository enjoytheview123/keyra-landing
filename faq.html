Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about how Keyra works, privacy, and what to expect during early access.

How it works

What exactly does Keyra do? +

Keyra is a coordination platform. We make it simple for everyday people to request clinician-reviewed peptide support for energy, recovery, and balance. We partner with independent licensed clinicians and authorized compounding pharmacies. Keyra itself does not prescribe, diagnose, or dispense medication.

How does the process work? +

1) You complete a short interest quiz. 2) When limited access opens, a licensed clinician through our partner network can review your information. 3) If appropriate, your request is sent to an authorized compounding pharmacy. 4) A discreet package is delivered to your door. There is no video visit required in the standard flow.

What happens after I submit the quiz? +

You will be added to our early-access interest list. We will only contact you when limited spots become available or if we need a quick follow-up. There is no automatic enrollment and no commitment.

Privacy & Trust

Who reviews the information I submit? +

Independent licensed clinicians through our trusted clinical infrastructure partner. Keyra does not employ the clinicians and does not make clinical decisions. Everything remains private and secure.

Do you prescribe or ship the peptides yourselves? +

No. Keyra coordinates access only. We do not prescribe, dispense, or compound any medication. Prescribing decisions belong to independent licensed clinicians. Fulfillment is handled by authorized compounding pharmacies.

How private is my information? +

We take privacy seriously. The interest form collects only high-level information (no detailed medical history). We do not sell your personal information. You can read our full Privacy Policy for complete details.

Getting Started

Is there any commitment or subscription? +

No. Joining the early-access list creates no obligation. When access is available, you only pay for what you choose to order. There are no memberships, no automatic renewals, and no hidden fees.

Is this available in my state? +

Availability depends on clinician licensing and pharmacy regulations, which vary by state. When you join the interest list we ask for your state so we can better understand where access can be offered as we expand.

Are the products high quality? +

Any products fulfilled through our partner network come from authorized compounding pharmacies that follow applicable quality and regulatory standards. Keyra does not manufacture or compound products itself.

Who is Keyra designed for? +

Keyra is built for everyday adults (typically 30–60+) who want simple, private support for energy, recovery, and balance — without complicated clinics or extreme protocols. It is not intended for medical emergencies or for anyone under 18.
