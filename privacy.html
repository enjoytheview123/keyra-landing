Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Keyra (“we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit keyrahealth.com or submit an interest form.

Information We Collect

When you complete our early-access interest form, we may collect:

  • First name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • State of residence
  • High-level preferences about wellness interests (energy, recovery, balance, etc.)

We do not collect detailed medical history, diagnoses, symptoms, medications, or other protected health information through this interest form.

How We Use Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Add you to our early-access / interest list
  • Contact you when limited access becomes available
  • Understand general interest trends
  • Improve our website and communications

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate our website or manage communications, only as needed to perform those services.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you submit. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Your Choices

You may request to be removed from our interest list at any time by contacting us at the email address below or by replying to any message we send.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the website or at the email address associated with your inquiry.

This policy applies to the interest form and general website use only. If you later engage with licensed clinicians through our partner network, additional privacy notices may apply.
